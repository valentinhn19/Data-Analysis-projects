Plate I

The Map

Every song reduced from nine audio features to two dimensions (PCA), and colored by the region it was assigned. Tight color clumping shows where the ear — and the algorithm — agree.

Plate II

Six Regions

Each region is a K-Means cluster fit on nine standardized audio features (danceability, energy, loudness, speechiness, acousticness, instrumentalness, liveness, valence, tempo).

Plate III

Sound Profile

Seven defining traits per region, each already on a natural 0–1 scale. The shape of each line is the region's signature.

Plate IV

Genre Composition

Regions are built from sound alone, yet they line up strongly with labeled genre (χ² test of independence, p ≈ 4.8×10⁻⁵¹) — while still cutting across genre, since pop appears in every region.

Plate V

Popularity, By Region

Average Spotify popularity score per region.

Plate VI

Chart a Course

Pick a starting point below. The recommender finds its region, then ranks the nearest coordinates within that region by cosine similarity on the same nine audio features used to draw the map.

Source: Spotify audio-feature data, 1,941 tracks (1998–2020), deduplicated from a 2,000-track sample. Clustering: K-Means, k=6, scikit-learn.
